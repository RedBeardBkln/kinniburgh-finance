## Defects / observations (none blocking; all low)

1. **Cosmetic duplicate wording on the Forecast Income table for a gross-unknown source.** `app/forecast/page.tsx` prefixes "Gross used, take-home unknown: " to `netInfo.label`, which already reads "gross $9,000.00 used, take-home unknown: confirm a paystub on the Income page", so the line reads "Gross used, take-home unknown: gross $9,000.00 used, take-home unknown: confirm ...". Repro: any source with `basis === "gross_unknown"` (none live; read the JSX at the table row). Expected: the phrase once.
2. **Stale copy next to the changed semantics (plan scoped the Settings page as unchanged, so not a criterion failure).** `app/settings/income-sources/page.tsx` still says "Recurring income events used by the 30-day forecast and low-balance alerts" with an unlabelled "Amount" column and the add/edit forms say "Amount ($)", while the forecast now uses take-home; the Forecast add form was relabelled "Gross amount per paycheck". The doc comment of `syncPaystubToIncomeSource` (`actions/paystubs.ts` lines 102-107) still says the forecast "reflects ... net take-home per paycheck" and then "The gross amount is used ..." (the stored amount is gross, as intended). Suggest relabelling "Gross per paycheck" on those forms/column and fixing the comment.
3. **Monthly review page still lists the bill record's day.** `lib/monthly-review-build.ts` / `app/review/[year]/[month]/page.tsx` ("Upcoming bills") show Solar's day 17 while every forecast surface uses the 14th. Plan explicitly scoped it out ("left as the bill record"); flagged for owner awareness because the stated owner rule is "bills default to the Budget's date".
4. **Guard regex gaps (static-scan limits).** Not caught: shorthand `select: { incomeSources }`, raw SQL on the IncomeSource table, aliasing (`const t = db.incomeSource; t.findMany`). Negligible for this codebase's style, listed for completeness.
5. **Source-read failure still rejects `checkLowBalance`** (so `/api/cron/notifications` returns 500 and `dispatchPending` is skipped, because the route uses `Promise.all`). Same failure mode as before this change (the sources were previously part of the account query), deposits/paystub failures are fail-soft. Also: a failed PAYSTUB read discards the successfully read deposits (all-or-nothing in one `try`), so net becomes flagged gross although deposits were readable; flagged, not silent.
6. **Ledger: a bill with no day of its own, dated only by Budget rows, produces no item (and no "Day not set" line) for a month beyond the Budget rows.** Repro (pinned by an OBSERVATION test): `autopayDay: null`, Budget rows for 2026-10 and 2026-11 only, 120-day window: items for Oct 14 and Nov 14, nothing for Dec/Jan. No live bill is in this state.
7. **Ledger: a monthly bill vs a Budget row of another frequency** (e.g. annual row with a pay day) no longer raises the day discrepancy and shows no date note (the bill's own day is used); the amount discrepancy still shows. Arguably right (the rows are not comparable), noted because the day signal disappears.
8. **N+1 loader calls**: `checkLowBalance` and `getEnvelopeForecastData` call `loadNetIncomeSources` once per account (3 queries each; 6 accounts live). Plan-specified; negligible at this size. The deposit read is `take: 400` newest inflows over the payroll accounts in 150 days (49 inflows live in 400 days); an account with more than 400 inflows could push payroll out of the window, which would degrade to stub/gross-unknown, flagged.
9. **Solar flips back to the 17th in January 2027** (no Budget row yet). Owner-approved fallback; the forecast chart shows the 14th for Oct-Dec and the 17th for Jan.
10. **Stub fallback accepts an employer-matching stub whose pay frequency differs from the source's** (gross must still match within 1%). The Tester brief wording ("gross within 1% and frequency") is stricter than the plan text and the implementation (employer OR frequency); harmless for per-paycheck net.
11. **Per-month annual/semiannual rows that disagree** (e.g. Oct row says payMonth 10, Nov row says payMonth 11) would each emit a due date in their own month inside one window. Live Amica/Progressive rows are identical across months, so no effect today; same family as the plan's "ScheduledBill is one row per category" risk. Also known and unchanged: Eva's forecast still counts a paycheck on 2026-10-09 whose deposit already arrived 10-07 until the owner moves her first paycheck date (reported, not changed, per plan).

Pre-existing and unrelated: the ledger's Doggy Daycare / Electric / Toyota / Firewood / McCarthy `monthly_amount` discrepancies (amount precedence unchanged by design) and the Lexus "history_differs" flag.
