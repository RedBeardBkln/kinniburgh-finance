# Plan: credit-card-full-pay (project every card's NEXT statements, detect paid statements, drop minimum payment)

## 1. Restated goal
Every card is paid in full each month, so the forecast, Credit Card Funding analysis, Upcoming ledger and card notifications must (a) include the statements that have not been issued yet as clearly labelled estimates, paid from the account that really pays each card, (b) stop calling a statement "may already be paid / unpaid" when the data shows it was paid, and (c) never surface or use the minimum payment.

## 3. Backtest (real history; owed series rebuilt from posted txs anchored on today's balance; n is tiny)
Predicting the statement paid in cycle k from information available N days before that cycle's close ("h"). "cum" = posted owed minus any still-unpaid earlier statement (charges already on the card this cycle). "cum+rate" = cum + trailing-90-day daily net charges x days to close. "last" = previous statement, "mean3" = average of previous 3.

| Estimator (mean abs % error) | Barclay n=5-6 | Capital One n=4 | jetBlue n=4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| last statement | 67% (worst 193%) | 142% (worst 484%) | 228% (worst 466%) |
| mean of last 3 | 88-101% (worst 226%) | 60-145% (worst 484%) | 207-259% |
| cum, h=0 (close day) | 0% ($3 constant) | 15% (worst 42%) | 0% |
| cum, h=10 | 8% (worst 34%, bias -$120) | 50% (under) | 0% |
| cum, h=20 | 46% (under) | 87% (under) | 23% |
| cum+rate, h=10 | 46% (OVER, +$476) | 26% | 84% (over) |
| cum+rate, h=20 | 81% (over) | 34% | 218% |

Conclusions: (1) previous-statement or average-of-statements are bad (spending is lumpy; 60-230% error) and must NOT be the primary estimate. (2) Within ~3 days of close, charges already posted are an excellent estimate (Barclay within $3). (3) The run-rate add-on is noisier than the shortfall it fixes (wins only for Capital One), so it is NOT added to the point estimate; it only sets the "could be up to" number shown in the note. (4) Beyond ~10 days from close, no tested estimator is better than ~25-90% error: those statements must be labelled rough, and the cycle-not-started statements (2 and 3 ahead) can only be a typical-month guess. Sample is 4-6 cycles per card, one household, a non-stationary trip-driven spend: report as indicative, not statistically meaningful.

## 4. Chosen estimator and exact algorithm (new pure module `lib/card-next-statement.ts`)

Inputs per card (all plain data; no DB): nickname, entityId, `currentBalance` (owed, positive), `currentBalanceAt`, `ccDueDate`, `ccStatementBalance`, `ccDataAt`, the card's non-archived transactions for the last 200 days (postedAt, amount, payeeNormalized, pending), non-card (checking/savings) outflow rows for the same span, `today` (America/New_York date as UTC midnight, `todayForNewYork`).

A. **Gates (any failure = no estimate, reason string shown once per card, never a guessed number)**
- `currentBalance` present and `currentBalanceAt` within 3 days of today; `ccDueDate` present and not older than 45 days (stale Plaid data).
- History: first posted tx at least 60 days old AND at least 2 observed payments (payment-like inflows) for statement #1; at least 3 for typical-month statements (#2, #3). Today: Barclay 7, Capital One 6, jetBlue 6 payments = all pass; a newly linked card would show "needs about 3 months of history".
- Reconcile check: if the on-file statement is unpaid and `currentBalance` < statement - $5, skip (balance and statement disagree).

B. **Paid detection of the on-file statement** (`detectStatementPaid`, also used by ledger/notifications): paid if, among POSTED card-account inflows dated from (dueDate - 14 days) to today, the sum of payment-like inflows (payeeNormalized matches `/payment|autopay|pymt/`) is >= statement - $0.01 (overpay counts); OR one inflow of >= statement - $0.01 has an outflow of the same amount (to the cent) on a non-card checking/savings account dated within -1..+5 days of it. Evidence returned: date, amount, which rule. Never "paid" without evidence; no evidence keeps today's wording. Works for a future due date too (early payment).

C. **Funding account** (`inferFundingAccount`): for the card's last 6 payment-like inflows, find outflows of the same amount to the cent on non-card checking/savings accounts dated -1..+5 days from the inflow (when more than one account matches, keep those whose payee matches `/barclay|crcardpmt|capital one|card|autopay/`; if still not exactly one account, skip that payment). Funding account = the account with the most matches, requiring at least 2 matches and at least 2/3 of the matched payments; otherwise `null` = "not determined": the card is excluded from per-account projections, listed once in a muted "funding account not determined for X" line, and still counted in the ledger. Live result: Barclay and Capital One -> Credit Cards, jetBlue -> Primary Checking. No hard-coded nickname.

D. **Statements to project**: a = on-file due day (UTC day-of-month). Generate monthly due dates after the on-file `ccDueDate` using the clamped helper (export a thin `monthlyDueDates(anchorDay, from, to)` wrapper over `allMonthDays` in lib/forecast.ts; no behaviour change). On-file unpaid statement and future due: counted as today (`scheduled`). On-file paid or past: dropped. Estimated dues: those with due in `[today, horizonEnd)`, up to 3.
- **#1 (open cycle)** `kind: "cycle_to_date"`, due D1 = on-file due + 1 month. `cum = currentBalance - (onfile unpaid ? statementBalance : 0)`; if `cum <= 0`: no item. Amount = `cum` (rounded to cents). `closeDate = D1 - 25 days`; `daysToClose = max(0, closeDate - today)`. Confidence `high` if daysToClose <= 3, `medium` if <= 10, else `low`. Also compute `upTo = cum + r x daysToClose` (r = trailing 90 days of posted non-payment, non-pending net charges per day, floored at 0, min 30 days of span) and put "could reach about $upTo if spending continues at its recent pace" in the note when daysToClose > 0. Today: Barclay Nov 5, $2,914.91, high (about 2 days to close); Capital One Nov 12, $72.56 (865.24 - 792.68), daysToClose about 15 -> low (backtest 50-87% under: say so); jetBlue Nov 12, $21.26 (72.52 - 51.26), low.
- **#2, #3 (cycle not started)** `kind: "typical_month"`, due = next months. Amount = MEDIAN of the last 3 observed payments (statement amounts as actually paid), range text from min/max of the last 6. Confidence always `low`. Needs >=3 payments.
- Labels: "Barclay statement due (estimate)"; why strings, plain and observational: "about $2,915: what is already charged this cycle; the statement closes in about 2 days" / "typical month: the middle of your last 3 payments ($X); payments over the last 6 months ranged $A to $B" / "at least $X already charged; the statement closes in about 15 days, more may post". No guarantee language.

E. **Counting policy (recommended default; owner question Q2)**: the owner pays in full, so every estimate IS counted in the Personal cash-flow forecast, funding analysis and ledger totals, but flagged `estimate` (ledger uses existing `outflowEstimated`). Notifications act only on `high`/`medium` estimates and on on-file statements (so rough guesses never page anyone).

## 9. Risks / unknowns
- R1 Estimator accuracy is only proven near close. With 4-6 cycles per card there is no statistical power; far-out statements (anything >10 days before close, and all typical-month ones) were 25-230% off in backtest. They are labelled rough on purpose. The plan does NOT claim better. Capital One's close lag is unknown (assumed 25 days); Plaid's `last_statement_issue_date` would remove the guess but is not stored (needs a column: out of scope, recommendation only).
- R2 Plaid lag: the cycle-to-date for #1 includes a few charges that post after the true close when Plaid has not yet rolled the statement (Barclay today, about +$54 / 2%). Accepted and visible in the why-note.
- R3 `analyzeCardFunding` ignores money moving INTO the funding account (scheduled transfers/income). With much larger projected payments it will over-report shortfalls unless inflows are passed in. I did NOT verify which ScheduledTransfer/IncomeSource rows feed the Credit Cards account; the Coder must check and pass `transfer_in` events for that account, otherwise gate funding notifications on estimates off (already the case for low confidence) and keep the page warning labelled "before scheduled transfers".
- R4 Entity separation: Capital One (EK Consulting) is paid from a Personal account. This plan does not change entity assignment of any transaction; it shows the cash leaving the Personal account and keeps the ledger item on the card's entity. Whether the owner reimburses personal via the Cottage account is not modelled (ground rule 6 says explicit transfer); asking, not guessing (Q3).
- R5 Matching heuristics assume the synced rows are complete; a missed sync can make a real payment look absent (then wording stays "no payment found yet", never "unpaid"). Overdue notification text is softened accordingly.
- R6 Payment-like regex is tuned to the three issuers seen; a new card/bank wording can fail paid detection (safe direction: stays "may already be paid") or funding inference (safe: "not determined").
- R7 The Barclay "may already be paid" box test strings are pinned by `upcoming-ledger-render.test.tsx` and `upcoming-ledger-tester.test.ts`; the change must be additive.
- R8 `Transaction.payeeNormalized` on Primary Checking mixes raw bank text with clean names (see memory); only "barclays"/"card" substrings are used here, not full canonical names.

## Errata (Coder, Round 1)
- Sections 4 and 8 call the Capital One and jetBlue Nov 12 estimates "low" with "daysToClose about 15". By the plan's own rule (close = due - lag; 3 / 10 day thresholds) with lag 25 they are 9 days from close = MEDIUM. The implementation follows the rule.
- The fixed 25-day close lag did not reproduce the section 3 claim "Barclay within $3 at close" (re-done backtest: 45% mean error at lag 25, 0% at lag 27). The implementation now infers the close lag per card from its own past cycles (`inferCloseLag`: at least 3 conclusive cycles within +-1 day, at least 2 narrow, none disagreeing, amounts within $5) and otherwise assumes 25 days with confidence capped at medium. Live today: Barclay 27 (inferred), Capital One and jetBlue not inferable (25 assumed, medium). The 0% figure for Barclay is in-sample on 5 cycles and is NOT a claim of future accuracy.
