## Required changes (blocking)

**R1. Do not label the Barclay-type estimate "high confidence" in owner-visible text; say what the number is and what can move it.**
The hold-out finding (lag inferred 26, not 27, in both testable out-of-sample cycles; in the Oct cycle a one-day lag error moved a ~$956 charge across the close, prediction $1,579 vs $623 paid) means the lag rule (accepts +-1 day per cycle) does not support a precision word. "High" here only means "the close date is believed to be about now". The word is printed, as a bare quality claim, in three places:
- `app/forecast/page.tsx` ~line 867: `{card.estimate.confidence} confidence: {card.estimate.why}`.
- `lib/upcoming-ledger.ts` (section 2b): note `Estimate, ${e.confidence} confidence: the statement has not been issued yet`.
- `lib/card-next-statement.ts` ~581-585: the `why` for the high case ("based on this cycle's charges so far; the statement closes about now (from your past statements)") reads as settled.
Required: keep the internal `high|medium|low` tier (it gates notifications and is tested) but stop printing the word for the owner. Print "estimate" plus the basis only. For `cycle_to_date` with the lag inferred, the `why` should carry the caveats, e.g. "based on charges posted so far this cycle; the statement is expected to close about now (the close date is worked out from your past statements and could be a day off), so charges posted or pending near the close can change the amount". Keep "could reach about $X" as is. The same `why` is reused verbatim inside the funding notification (`buildFundingMessage`: "an estimate <why>") and the ledger `tierNote`/advisor, so one fix covers all of them; re-read the notification sentence afterwards so the nested parentheses still read cleanly. Update the tests that pin the old strings (`card-next-statement.test.ts` ~lines 455-490, plus any in the tester files and `cc-funding.test.ts`/`notifications.test.ts` that match the `why`). No algorithm change needed for R1.

**R2. After the statement close date has passed (Plaid not yet rolled), the estimate must not say "closes about now" or stay high.**
`projectCardStatements` clamps `daysToClose = Math.max(0, ...)`, so once `closeDate < today` the text is still "the statement closes about now" and confidence stays `high` (test `card-next-statement.test.ts:455` pins "close has passed: 0 days -> high"). Plaid rolls the due date only "a few days after each close" (plan section 2), and until then `cum = currentBalance` keeps absorbing charges that belong to the FOLLOWING statement. Barclay's inferred close is today (Oct 9), so from tomorrow until Plaid rolls (live, within days of deploy) the owner would see "high confidence, closes about now" on a figure that is knowingly growing with next-cycle charges, and the daily funding alert quotes it. Required: when `closeDate` is before `today`, cap the tier at `medium` and use text such as "the statement should have closed N days ago; charges posted since may belong to the next statement, so this may be too high". (Excluding post-close charges from `cum` would be better but is a larger change; the cap + wording is enough now.) Update the pinned test accordingly.

**R3. The page's "(the amount includes estimated statements)" is false for the live shortfall.**
`app/forecast/page.tsx` shortfall box appends that parenthetical whenever `estimatedTotalDue > 0`. But `shortfall` is the FIRST dip only (`analyzeCardFunding`: set once, on the first day the balance is below the minimum). Live: the $543.59 figure is Capital One's on-file $792.68 on Oct 12 alone (499.09 - 792.68 = -293.59; 250 + 293.59); the Barclay estimate lands Nov 5 and is only in the "keeps falling ... $3,458.50 in total" sentence. So on deploy day the page tells the owner the $543.59 includes estimated statements when it does not. Required: show the parenthetical only when an estimated due is on or before `firstShortfallDate` (e.g. `fa.cards.some(c => c.estimate && c.dueDate <= fa.firstShortfallDate)`), otherwise omit it (the peak sentence already carries the estimate caveat; check that sentence says the total includes estimates). `buildFundingMessage` does not have this defect (its "Estimated amounts are not final..." line is generic).
