## Deviations from the plan
1. Paid window starts 21 days before the due date (plan: 14). 21 is the legal minimum between statement and due date, so an early payment right after the statement is detected and the previous cycle's late payment (<= due-23d even in February) is still excluded. Direction of any error is "not paid".
2. Plan's illustrative "Capital One / jetBlue Nov 12 daysToClose ~15, low" is arithmetic-inconsistent with its own algorithm: Nov 12 minus 25 days = Oct 18, 9 days from today, so the stated thresholds give MEDIUM. Implemented the algorithm and thresholds exactly; the note text for medium/low says "only the charges so far ... more may post" and shows "could reach about $X". Capital One's close lag is still an unpinned assumption (backtest was 50-87% under at this distance) - see Open items.
3. Added `peakShortfall` / `peakShortfallDate` to `analyzeCardFunding` (not in the plan). With estimates the old "shortfall" (amount needed on the FIRST dip only) under-states the need (live: $543.59 vs $3,458.50), so the UI and message now state both. `shortfall` itself is unchanged.
4. Added `\bbarclays\b|\bautopay pymt\b` to `SKIP_PAYEE_RE` in `lib/recurring-detect.ts`: the live checking payee for Barclay payments is "barclays", which the existing list (barclaycard, crcardpmt) did not cover, so the card payment itself would have been offered as a recurring bill next to the card estimate (double count, request item 1). Verified before/after with a probe and pinned by tests; an ordinary monthly bill is still suggested.
5. Typical-month estimates require 3 payments after clustering split payments (rows within 7 days sum to one payment) - small addition so a split payment cannot fake history.
6. On projection load failure the pages drop card payments and show a notice (plan said fall back to on-file-only with the old hard-coded account); an assumed account would contradict "never assigned". Notifications return 0 on load failure rather than guess.
7. Funding view renders one card per paying account (so jetBlue/Primary Checking gets its own), not only the "Credit Cards" account.
8. Tests: ledger render/ledger tests are `.tsx` (static markup) instead of `.ts`; forecast helper tests are in a new file rather than editing `forecast.test.ts` (CRLF).

### D3 - per-card close-lag inference (implemented; it works for Barclay only)
- `inferCloseLag({ anchorDay, txs })` in `lib/card-next-statement.ts` (pure). Each past payment is attached to the nearest monthly due date; for each lag 15..32 the net charges posted in (previous due - lag, due - lag] are compared with that payment ($5 tolerance). A cycle that fits no lag is inconclusive; the history must reach back past the earliest candidate close. A lag is accepted only when >= 3 conclusive cycles fit it within +-1 day, >= 2 of those are narrow (fit <= 4 lags, so they really locate the close), and NO conclusive cycle disagrees; ties go to the lag that fits the most cycles exactly, then the one nearest 25. Otherwise null.
