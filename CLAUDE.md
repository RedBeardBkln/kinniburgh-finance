# Kinniburgh Financial Platform

Interactive personal + business financial management platform for Eric Kinniburgh and Eva-Laura Ramirez-Wisiackas. Covers envelope budgeting, spend analysis, forecasting, project budgets, retirement planning, business bookkeeping for three LLC/entities, tax document organization and prep, and life insurance policy storage/analysis.

## Ground rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Never fabricate financial data.** Every account number, dollar amount, date, and rule in these specs comes from the owner's source documents. If something is missing or ambiguous, check `specs/07-source-data-notes.md`; if still unresolved, ask the user — do not invent a value.
2. **Money is stored as integer cents** (or `NUMERIC(14,2)` in Postgres). Never floats.
3. **Transactions are immutable facts.** Tags, categories, and budget assignments are mutable metadata layered on top. Support audit history.
4. **Two-person household.** Eric and Eva are separate users with shared visibility; notifications can target either or both.
5. **Security first.** This app holds real bank data. Encrypt secrets at rest, never log account numbers or tokens, use OAuth/Plaid tokens (never store bank passwords), enforce auth on every route, support MFA.
6. **Personal vs. business separation is a core invariant.** Every transaction belongs to exactly one bucket (Personal, Sudden Valley Property Management LLC, Eric Kinniburgh Consulting LLC, or Mezzo). Cross-bucket flows (e.g., reimbursements via THE COTTAGE account) are modeled as explicit transfers, not edits.
7. **Tax data retention.** Nothing tax-related is ever hard-deleted; archive only.
8. **Tax output is a computed return prepared by the owner; no investment advice.** Eric Kinniburgh is the self-preparer of record of the TY2025 return and there is no CPA review step (his CPA retired). The tax side SHOULD compute amounts, credits and eligibility conclusions from the owner's answers, the documents and the books — do not water them down to "observations" or add blanket "never decides" restrictions — and produces a complete filing package that the owner reviews, approves and signs himself. The AI Return Reviewer (see "Return review and approval") is advisory: it is software, NOT a CPA, EA or other licensed professional; it can reduce risk but cannot guarantee correctness or acceptance; no output (UI, sheet, cover, PDF, package, spec) may claim professional certification, licensure, or that the return is "CPA approved" or "professionally reviewed"; its PASSED result is a prerequisite for, never a substitute for, the owner's own attestation, which only his account can record. The clean (unstamped) copies and the final package exist only for a return the owner approved at its current fingerprint, say "Prepared by Eric Kinniburgh (self-prepared)", and never mention Claude, AI or this app. Guardrails: (a) every rule, threshold and table used in a computation, and every legal claim in a review finding, must be primary-sourced (irs.gov / Connecticut DRS) and cited in code and `specs/09-*`, or marked "unverified"; a rule that cannot be verified is flagged "needs a professional's input" on that line (internal status `needs_cpa_*`), never guessed or approximated; (b) every computed line carries provenance (which document / answer / books entry, and whether it is verified or an unverified AI read) and every pre-approval output is labelled a draft; (c) overrides are recorded and shown, never applied silently; where the law leaves a real choice (e.g. standard vs actual home-office method), show the computed alternatives side by side — the choice is the owner's. Non-tax guidance (spending, budgets, investing) stays data-driven observation, not investment or financial advice ("you've spent 80% of the Groceries budget").

## Architecture

**Stack:** Next.js 15 App Router · TypeScript · Prisma/PostgreSQL (Supabase) · NextAuth v5 · Plaid · Tailwind · shadcn/ui · Vitest

**Server actions** (`actions/*.ts`) — all use `"use server"` and call `requireAuth()` as the first line; never skip it. `requireAuth()` throws if no session.

**Auth** — NextAuth v5 credentials provider (email + password + optional TOTP). Session cookie: `authjs.session-token` (HTTP) / `__Secure-authjs.session-token` (HTTPS). Middleware in `middleware.ts` gates all routes except `/login`. **Deliberate exception:** the public `/queue/[token]` review page (Eva's "assign to Eva" magic link) is exempt in middleware, and every export in `actions/review-queue.ts` is token-gated by `requireReviewAccess(token)` (validated against the DB on each call) instead of `requireAuth()`; this is intentional because the caller has no NextAuth session, so it must never be "fixed" to use `requireAuth()` or have the gate removed. Its security boundary is that per-action token check (not the middleware exemption), and a test pins that every export starts with it.

**Entities** — the core multi-tenancy unit. Personal + three LLCs (Sudden Valley, EK Consulting, Mezzo). Entity slug drives the URL (`/business/[slug]/...`). `getNavBuckets()` in `lib/entity.ts` builds the header tabs.

**Money** — `NUMERIC(14,2)` in Postgres; `Decimal.js` at runtime. **Negative = outflow, positive = inflow.** Never use floats for amounts.

**"income" vs "revenue"** — `GlCode.type` (business entities) uses `revenue` (the enum is `asset|liability|equity|revenue|expense`, defined in `actions/gl-codes.ts`'s `GL_TYPES` — never `income`, confirmed against live production data). `"income"` is reserved for personal-finance concepts (e.g. `lib/forecast.ts`'s `ScheduleEventType`, `lib/tax-guidance.ts`'s question categories). This exact mix-up recurred across three separate tasks before being fixed for good in the `gl-code-tag-mapping` change — grep for `"income"` near any `GlCode`/`glCodeId`/`computePL` code before assuming it's correct.

**Encryption** — `lib/encrypt.ts`: AES-256-GCM, format `iv:authTag:ciphertext` (hex). Used for Plaid access tokens, cursors, vault credentials. Key from `ENCRYPTION_KEY` env var (64-char hex → 32 bytes).

**Plaid sync** — `lib/plaid-sync.ts`: use `normalizePlaidTransaction()` for all Plaid tx conversion (flips sign, handles payee priority). Use `normalizePayee()` from `lib/tags.ts` (not `.toLowerCase()`) for `payeeNormalized` field.

**Soft deletes** — `archivedAt: null` guards required on all `findUnique`/`findFirst` queries for Transaction, Account, PlaidItem, VaultOtp. Tax records are never hard-deleted.

**Vault** — separate OTP + session mechanism (`VaultOtp`, `VaultSession`); `lib/vault-session.ts` validates the `vault-session` cookie (4h expiry). OTP has `attempts` column; burns after 5 failures (429).

**Tags** — `lib/tags.ts`: hierarchical (parent/children paths like "Food & Drink / Groceries"); `matchTagRule()` for auto-assignment; always use `normalizePayee()` for `payeeNormalized`.

**Document extraction** — `Document.extractionStatus === "complete"` means only that the AI run finished; "verified" is `extractionConfirmedAt` being set, and owner corrections live in an overlay column `extractionCorrections` (the AI's `extractionData` is never edited). `TAX_EXTRACTION_POLICY` (`lib/tax-extraction-policy.ts`) defaults to `verified_else_ai` (unverified AI reads are used but labelled). Compute/Forms code must read tax extraction data only through `resolveTaxDocForCompute` / `resolveEffectiveExtraction`, never raw `extractionData`.

**TY2025 return engine** — `lib/tax2025/**` is pure (no DB, network or clock): `resolveFacts()` turns rows into `Ty2025Facts` + conflicts + open items, `computeTy2025Return(facts, decisions)` assembles every `LineKey` (`line-catalog.ts`) with an explicit status via rule modules in `rules/`. Every tax number lives ONLY in `constants.ts` (each with irs.gov / ct.gov url + verifiedOn; unverified rules return `needs_cpa_rule_unverified`, never an estimate), and a test fails if a rule file repeats one. A rare line is `not_applicable` 0 only when the owner stated "none" for its group (`facts.statedNone`), otherwise `not_yet_computed`: never a silent 0. A decision keyed by a list of GL accounts (`lib/tax2025/business-use.ts`, X6 ...) applies an owner-attested business-use percentage to a mixed-use account (undecided = 100%, flagged default, undecided). `lib/tax2025-build.ts` is the only DB-aware file (read-only; no auth, so callers use `requireAuth()` and pass only `ret` to clients). `lib/tax-compute.ts` keeps the CT tables and v1 helpers.

**TY2025 PDF packet** — `lib/tax2025/pdf/**` turns a computed `Ty2025Return` into a DRAFT zip of filled blank IRS forms (+ the CT-1040 as a flat form with our own overlay fields) and a cover page (the owner's internal workfile; the draft cover is a separate download and is never inside the final zip); it only consumes the engine (the engine never imports it; a test pins this) and never recomputes a number. `adapter.ts` is the only file that reads engine shapes; `maps/*.ts` map every AcroForm field to an engine `LineKey`, an answer, a header name or an explicit `blank` reason (a completeness test requires each field to be claimed exactly once). Invariants: the routes `app/api/tax/forms/[year]/pdf/**` start with `auth()` and return 401 before anything else, nothing is persisted (one `AuditLog` row with ids and counts only), every string written to a PDF or the cover goes through `safeText` (SSN-like text is refused), SSN/EIN/bank/PIN/signature/preparer/address/DOB fields are always blank, and the blank-not-zero policy holds (a line that could not be computed is blank and listed on the cover, never "0"; a form the engine requires but has no map is listed under "Required forms this packet does NOT contain"; a type / description / code box beside a zero amount line is the blank reason `zero_line_entry` with `follows`, and if a followed line ever carries an amount the packet raises an advisory item and L1 raises `L1.B5.entry-by-hand` (the Form 1040 line 16 and Schedule 2 line 4 boxes, beside lines that are normally not zero, are `whenOverridden`: raised only if that line is overridden): no printed text line may be silently `not_modeled`, a ratchet test pins 0 unmapped on every form). Which forms are included follows `Ty2025Return.formsRequired`. Schedule 1-A (every printed money line is an engine line) and Form 8960 (lines 1-17 for an individual) have maps, and so does Form 8606 (one sheet per person through `FormCopy.lines`; lines 1, 2, 3 and 14 only: a distribution or conversion blocks instead of printing); Form 8829 is intentionally NOT generated (the engine states whether it is required: not under the simplified home-office method, listed on the cover as not generated if the owner chooses the actual method). The per-page stamp reads `DRAFT - not approved for filing - <date> - fp <fp12>` and says nothing about who computed or checked it. Clean copies (`?stamp=0`) and the final package (`?final=1`: `00-package-index.pdf` + `forms/` + `attachments/`, no stamp, neutral document properties) are served only after `auth()` AND only when an owner approval exists for the CURRENT return fingerprint (`lib/tax2025-pdf-approval.ts` `ApprovalLookup`; a failed lookup is 403, never a clean copy); every string we write into the final package is scanned against the banned-wording list in `lib/tax-wording.ts` and a hit fails the build closed. Blank forms are pinned by sha256 in `data/forms/manifest.json` (a re-issued form is a deliberate, reviewed change; `data/forms` is about 3 MB).

**TY2025 owner overrides and CT-1040 lines** — owner overrides (new ones are always recorded with `authority: "owner"`; the DB column default stays `cpa` under the additive-only schema policy and stored `cpa` rows display as "Advisor (recorded earlier)"; a LINE override in force blocks owner approval) live in the append-only `TaxReturnOverride` table (versioned; a change inserts version+1 and archives the old row, clearing only archives; never hard-deleted; reason text is a tax record and never goes into `AuditLog`, which carries ids/counts only). `lib/tax2025/overrides.ts` is pure: an override REPLACES one line's value in the effective view only; every dependent line (edges in `lib/tax2025/line-flow.ts`, kept honest by key-existence, acyclic and spine-drift tests) is flagged "depends on an override" and is NOT recomputed, `headline.complete` is false while a pin is in force, a pin on a blocked line unblocks that line only, and a stale pin (value or status changed) is blocking while an engine-version-only change is advisory. Decisions (`Ty2025Decisions`) still recompute the whole return. The sheet, CSV and PDF packet must all load through `lib/tax2025-overrides-build.ts` (`buildTy2025ReturnWithOverrides`, DB-aware, fail-closed: unreadable rows return an error, never the un-overridden return) and show one note string (`formatOverrideNote`); the actions in `actions/tax-return-overrides.ts` start with `requireAuth()` and refuse SSN-like reasons. When the engine gains or changes a line that feeds another, add its edge to `line-flow.ts` or the drift test fails. CT-1040: lines 3, 7, 8, 12-14, 16, 17, 20a-d, 21, 22, 25, 26, 29, 30 and Schedule 3/4 detail are engine lines (`rules/ct.ts`, `ct-credits.ts`, `ct-settlement.ts`); lines 7, 13 and 20a-d are gated by owner none-statements (unanswered = blocking, never a silent 0), lines 23 and 25 and Form 1040 lines 35a / 36 follow the owner decisions X8 / X7 (refund all / apply all / apply a stated whole-dollar amount; undecided = printed blank, flagged default; `lib/tax2025/overpayment.ts`, `rules/overpayment-split.ts`) while lines 24 / 24a are never modelled (recording X8 states that none is made), Schedule 3 is left blank (with a cover note) when the property tax credit is fully phased out, and PDF text that may not fit a cell goes through `pdf/fit-text.ts` (shortened or smaller font, full text kept in an advisory, never a silent clip).

**Return review and approval** (`specs/11-return-review-and-self-preparation.md`) — the owner-approval flow that gates clean copies. A review run and an approval are bound to the return fingerprint v2 (`lib/tax-review/fingerprint.ts`: engine version + view fingerprint + answers + printed header + facts + document set with verification/corrections + questionnaires + overrides + decisions, recomputed on the server for every request; clients never supply it); any change makes the run and any approval non-current. Three layers, each producing structured findings (`lib/tax-review/types.ts`): L1 deterministic invariants (`l1/`: footing per form, PDF read-back vs engine, source-document tie-out, process state, privacy guard), L2 an independent recomputation (`l2/`, shares no rule code with the engine: only `constants.ts`, the fact/line types and the effective-override view), L3 focused LLM passes (`llm/`, Anthropic server-side only; model `TAX_REVIEW_MODEL`, default `claude-opus-5-5`; price vars optional; payer names are sent by default and `TAX_REVIEW_PAYER_NAMES=generic` hides them; household names, addresses, own entity names, SSNs and account numbers are never sent). The gate and the "AI review: PASSED / FLAGGED" verdict are computed by code in `lib/tax-review/gate.ts`, never by a model: an LLM can only ADD findings (a finding that cites a line or value that does not exist is rejected; a law claim without a checkable quote from the pinned `data/tax-sources/2025` pack is `unverified`, capped at medium, and still needs the owner's written acknowledgement) and can never close one. There is NO waiver: L1, L2 and L3 must all have run for the CURRENT fingerprint, a decision still at its default alternative gates, a line override in force gates, and a layer that has not run, failed or is partial keeps the gate red. Only the owner's account (the user named in the EK Consulting entity name; ambiguity means nobody) can accept a finding (written reason) or approve, with the attestation text v2 (`ATTESTATION_V2_TEXT`, hash stored with version id), the typed phrase and his full name; this is recorded as `APPROVED BY OWNER`. An approval stops counting, without being deleted, when the fingerprint changes, when a blocking finding is open again or was decided again after it, or when an AI review was cancelled after it (`currentApproval` / `approvalRevocationReasons`, read by the page and the PDF routes through ONE reader, `lib/tax-review-approval-facts.ts`; any read failure, including a missing table, fails closed: 403). `TaxReviewRun`, `TaxReviewFinding`, `TaxReviewFindingDisposition`, `TaxReturnApproval` and `TaxReviewRunEvent` are append-only (the store modules have no update/delete/upsert path; a source-reading test pins it); findings, reasons and names never contain SSN-like text, and `AuditLog` rows carry ids, counts, fingerprints and hashes only, never values or reasons. Server actions (`actions/tax-review.ts`, `actions/tax-return-approval.ts`) start with `requireAuth()`; spending API credit and recording a decision are owner-only; Anthropic errors log `err.name` only, never prompts or findings. All owner-visible wording passes `lib/tax-wording.ts` (rewrites "the CPA decides" prose at the render boundary; internal ids such as `needs_cpa_*`, `answer_cpa`, `cpaNote` and the `/cpa-summary` URL stay). The reviewer's text must never say it is a CPA, and the honesty panel sits directly above the approval card.

**Tax facts carry-forward store** (`lib/tax-facts/**`, `lib/tax-facts-store.ts`, `actions/tax-facts.ts`, page `/tax/facts`; table `TaxFact`, migration `20261010000000_tax_facts`) — the owner's confirmed facts (spec 12) kept as append-only versioned rows so a new year can stop re-asking them (per fact, on the carry screen). Each fact has a stable dotted key, a typed value (`text|choice|bool|percent|money_cents|none_statement|open_item`, money in integer cents), the tax year that version was confirmed for, a source (`owner_statement` is shown as "not verified by documents") and a carry-forward policy: `stable` (carries until changed), `reconfirm` (carries as a suggestion the owner must confirm; the default whenever unsure), `year_specific` (never carries, asked fresh), `derived` (a closing figure that becomes the next opening value). A change, a re-confirmation for a later year, a policy change, a retirement and the resolution of an open item are each a NEW version (`archivedAt` is set on the previous one; the only update ever made; there is no delete, `deleteMany` or `upsert` on `taxFact`, a test pins it). `resolveCarryForward(rows, year)` (pure) splits facts into carried / needs re-confirmation / open items / ask fresh / already confirmed for the year; it never marks a carried fact confirmed and never invents a value. The seed (`seed-ty2025.ts`, loaded by an owner button, idempotent: inserts only keys with no row) is transcribed from spec 12 and every entry carries a `specAnchor` that must be a verbatim sentence of that file (a test reads the spec). Text fields are refused if they look like an SSN, EIN, account number or date of birth; `AuditLog` rows carry ids, key, version, category, policy, kind of change, year and value type only, never a label, value or reason. The store is NOT read by the TY2025 engine, the return fingerprint or the AI reviewer, and none of `lib/tax2025/**` or `lib/tax-review/**` may import it (a test pins both directions); generating `OWNER_STATEMENTS_*` from the store and pre-filling questionnaires from carried facts are deferred. **The carry screen `/tax/facts/carry/[year]` exists: it lists the resolver's buckets for a target year (2026 or later; TY2025 and earlier are refused before any database call), `reconfirmTaxFact` and `changeTaxFactForCarry` (`actions/tax-facts-carry.ts`) are its only writers, one fact per call, with no bulk confirm and decisions never grouped. It is still a recall and re-confirmation aid: nothing (questionnaires, engine, reviewer, fingerprint) consumes the store, so do not describe it as feeding any return.** The page fails closed with a "migration not applied" message while the table does not exist.

**Tax year close** (`lib/tax-year-close/**`, `lib/tax-year-close-store.ts`, `lib/tax-year-close-owner.ts`, `actions/tax-year-close.ts`; table `TaxYearCloseEvent`, migration `20261011000000_tax_year_close_events`, NOT applied until the owner OKs it) — the owner marks a household tax year filed ("closed") and can reopen it for revision. It is a household-level, insert-only event log on the Personal entity (a close and a reopen are each a new row with seq+1; the unique `(entityId, taxYear, seq)` index makes two racing tabs safe; current state = the highest seq: none = open, closed = filed, reopened = open for revision; never updated or deleted, a source-reading test pins it). Only the owner's own account can close or reopen (`resolveYearCloser` reuses the approver rule); anyone logged in sees the state. The date filed, an optional note and the required reopen reason are stored; there is NO column for a confirmation number and SSN/EIN/account-like text is refused; the note and reason are tax records and never go into `AuditLog` (ids, year, seq and kind only). It is a SOFT label: the badge/banner (`components/tax/year-state-badge.tsx`, `year-status-notice.tsx`) appears on the Tax workspaces list, the Tax Forms hub (with the owner card `year-close-card.tsx`), the questionnaire, return sheet, final review and summary pages, the personal, donations and fixed-assets pages and the business workspace page, and it renders nothing (never breaks a page) when the table is missing or unreadable (display reads are fail-soft). No existing tax action checks it. The only hard guard is on the carry screen: `lib/tax-facts-carry-guard.ts` refuses a carry target at or before the latest closed year (a reopened year does not block), and its read of the close events is fail-closed. `TaxWorkspace.status` / `filedAt` and `TaxDeadline.status` are separate per-entity / per-deadline labels, are not read or written by this feature, and are not the source of truth for the household year. The close state is never read by the engine, the return fingerprint, the PDF routes, the AI reviewer or the approval flow (tests pin it), so closing a year changes no number, form, PDF or approval.

**Financial assistant (`/advisor`)** (`lib/advisor/**`, `actions/advisor.ts`, `app/api/advisor/chat/route.ts`, `components/advisor/**`; tables `AdvisorConversation`, `AdvisorMessage`, `AdvisorMemory`, `AdvisorUsage`, migration `20261012000000_advisor_assistant`, NOT applied until the owner OKs it; plans and reviews in `.claude/pipeline/advisor-ai-chatbot/` and `advisor-ai-chatbot-phase2/`; Phase 2 has NO migration) � a streaming chat over the platform's own data. 24 READ-ONLY tools (7 money, 6 TY2025 tax, 4 business and planning: entity P&L, rental income, recurring/scheduled items, forecast; 4 records: documents, donations, fixed assets, insurance; 2 state and change: tax calendar, recent changes; `get_document_values`) plus `propose_memory_note` = 25, registry in `lib/advisor/tools/all-tools.ts`. The model has no write path: `propose_memory_note` only VALIDATES a suggestion (the person's CURRENT message must contain a remember-style trigger and the exact quoted phrase, at most 2 per answer, never because a tool result says so, a test feeds a poisoned payee to a fake model) and the loop emits a `memory_proposal` event that the UI shows with Save / Dismiss; only the click calls `confirmMemorySuggestion` in `actions/advisor.ts` (requireAuth, re-scrubbed, 50-note cap, id-only AuditLog, author = the person who clicked). Memory is otherwise added/forgotten only by the signed-in user. Only `lib/advisor/store.ts` writes; conversations are private to their user (every query carries `userId`; a missing, archived or foreign conversation is the same 404), messages and usage are insert-only, memory and conversations are archive-only. The route starts with `auth()` (401); every action starts with `requireAuth()`. Everything the model sees or the user is shown passes `lib/advisor/scrub.ts` (reuses the AI Return Reviewer's `findRedactionIssues`: SSN/EIN/account numbers, e-mails and dates of birth never leave) and `wording.ts` (same banned-wording rules as the tax side; the assistant is software, not a CPA, and gives data-driven observation, not investment advice). Tools use explicit `select`s (a test enforces it) and never import the tax engine's writers, server actions or `next/cache`; nothing in `lib/tax2025/**` or `lib/tax-review/**` imports advisor code (a test pins it). The only SDK file is `lib/advisor/anthropic.ts` (beta namespace; strict tool schemas are OFF by default because 25 tools do not fit any strict limit and every call is re-validated by zod server-side; with `ADVISOR_STRICT_TOOLS=1` it still degrades without `strict` then without `fallbacks` on a 400; logs `err.name` and a level number only). Daily caps are checked before a turn and usage is written after it, so concurrent sends can overshoot slightly. The page and store reads fail soft while the migration is missing (`/advisor` shows "not set up yet", chat answers 503). Env vars (all optional): `ADVISOR_MODEL` (default `claude-opus-5-5`), `ADVISOR_EFFORT` (`low|medium|high|xhigh|max`, default `medium`), `ADVISOR_STRICT_TOOLS=1` (opt in, default off) and `ADVISOR_FALLBACKS=0` (kill switch), `TAX_REVIEW_PAYER_NAMES=generic` (also hides payer, employer and charity names in `get_document_values`, like the AI Return Reviewer), `ADVISOR_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS`, `ADVISOR_MAX_TOOL_ITERATIONS`, `ADVISOR_TURN_TOKEN_CAP`, `ADVISOR_TURN_BUDGET_MS`, `ADVISOR_DAILY_TURNS` (default 40/user), `ADVISOR_HOUSEHOLD_DAILY_TURNS`, `ADVISOR_DAILY_TOKENS`; needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. Document values: `queries/document-values.ts` is the ONLY advisor file allowed to select the extraction columns (`ALLOWED_RAW_USES`; a test pins it), reads only through `resolveTaxDocForCompute`, and `tools/document-fields.ts` returns only registry-allowlisted fields (default deny: no EIN/mask/identifier keys, no addresses, no taxpayer name, text only for payer/employer/lender/issuer/charity/entity names); a value that would need scrubbing is withheld, not rewritten; every document is labelled verified or unverified AI read; non-tax and insurance-linked documents answer 'not available'. `redactStreetAddresses` (lib/advisor/scrub.ts) is a heuristic (number + street word + suffix) applied only to owner-typed descriptive fields; an address without a number passes. The `AppShell` mounts `components/advisor/advisor-launcher.tsx` for signed-in pages (hidden on /advisor and in print) which opens a non-modal slide-over sharing the chat hook; the client sends only the pathname and the server re-parses it against the closed table in `lib/advisor/page-context.ts` (a fixed page label and tax year, never an id, slug or query). The composer shows a usage line (questions left today, fresh tokens in the last 24 hours; counts only, never money) from `meta` and `actions/advisor-usage.ts`.

**Upcoming ledger** (`lib/upcoming-ledger.ts` pure builder, `lib/upcoming-ledger-view.ts` pure formatting, `lib/upcoming-ledger-build.ts` read-only loader; `components/upcoming/**`; the dashboard "Next 30 days" widget in `app/page.tsx` and the `#upcoming` 30/60/90-day agenda in `app/forecast/page.tsx`; no schema change) - one dated, de-duplicated, entity-scoped list of expected outflows and inflows. Date math is only `lib/forecast.ts`'s generators; the ledger decides precedence and totals. Same budget category (entity + tag): scheduled bill beats Budget schedule beats RecurringExpense; losers are kept as `alsoRecordedAs` and a differing monthly amount (more than $1) or day is shown as "records disagree" (the bill's figure is the one shown, the Budget row compared is the one for the item's own month). Untagged records that look like an already-counted obligation (shared distinctive name word + same account, or close amount + same day) are `heldBack`: visible, never counted; two untagged records of the same kind are never merged with each other. Never fabricated: an unknown amount is listed as "amount not set" (not $0, not dropped); an unknown pay day goes to `undated` ("Day not set", never guessed as the 1st, unlike the cash-flow charts). Envelope transfers are listed only on request and are never in the totals; a card statement up to 14 days past its due date is shown in a "may already be paid" box outside the totals; totals are per entity and the all-entities views show one line per entity, never a blended total. Tier `estimated` (accrual draws and spreads, fluctuating bills, projected revenue) carries an "estimate" badge; `learned` is defined but never produced. Month-length: `allMonthDays` (lib/forecast.ts) clamps a listed day 29-31 to the last day of a short month and collapses duplicates (e.g. [30, 31] gives one date), so month-end bills and paychecks are never skipped. A zero, null or negative amount on a paycheck, rental payout, projected revenue or envelope transfer is listed (date kept) as "Amount not set" and never counted or shown as $0.00. Both pages call it inside try/catch and render a small notice on failure.

**Testing pattern** — pure function unit tests in `lib/__tests__/`. Use `Decimal` (never floats). Test factory `makeTx(overrides)` for mock Plaid transactions. No integrated DB tests; mock at the function boundary.
