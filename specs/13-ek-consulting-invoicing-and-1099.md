## 1. Scope and ground rules

- Invoicing exists for the Eric Kinniburgh Consulting, LLC entity only (slug `ek-consulting`, dba LaunchTime Solutions). Gate = a code allow-list (`lib/invoicing/entity-gate.ts`) AND an enabled `InvoicingProfile` row.
- Invoices are ACCRUAL in the platform; the entity files on the CASH method. The accrual books are DERIVED from the documents (`lib/invoicing/ledger.ts`); no journal table, no `Transaction` is ever written. A bank deposit is LINKED to the payment it settles (`BillingPayment.depositTransactionId`); the ledger P&L (`computePL`), the TY2025 engine, the CPA bundle and the advisor are unchanged.
- The tax engine (`lib/tax2025/**`, `lib/tax-review/**`) and the advisor never import invoicing code (pin tests). Wiring a cash-basis view into TY2026 Schedule C is a separate, reviewed step (open question Q9).
- Owner-only operations (owner rule = the user whose full name is contained in the EK Consulting entity name; ambiguous = nobody, fail closed): EVERY write in the invoicing suite: drafting and editing invoices, clients and contacts, the catalog, issue, void, record / void payments, apply and remove applications, link deposits, settings, numbering, remit-to, EIN, reveal an EIN, 1099 PDFs. Eva (any signed-in non-owner) is READ-ONLY: she can view invoices, clients, items and payments and download an invoice PDF, sees an EIN as its last four digits only, and every mutating action refuses her (a guard test fails when an exported action lacks the gate; the pages hide the controls from a plain server-decided boolean). Scope note: this is the invoicing suite only; nothing else on the EK Consulting entity (transactions, tagging, budgets) changed.
- Money is integer cents; archive-only (the only delete is the line rows of a DRAFT document); every write is entity-scoped; `AuditLog` and `BillingEvent` carry ids, counts and cents only.

## 3. Verified outside facts

### IRS (primary sources)

- **A1 Threshold.** "Instructions for Forms 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC (12/2026)", https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099mec, "What's New": "For tax years beginning after 2025, the minimum threshold amount for reporting certain payments ... increased to $2,000 and may be adjusted for inflation beginning in calendar year 2027." File Form 1099-NEC for each person paid at least $2,000 for services performed by someone who is not an employee, or payments to an attorney; also regardless of amount if federal income tax was withheld (box 4). The 2025 (pre-change) amount was not read directly, so the code supports payment year 2026 only. verifiedOn 2026-10-15.
- **A2 Due dates.** Same page: file Form 1099-NEC and furnish payee statements on or before January 31; when that day is a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday the due date is the next business day. 2027-01-31 is a Sunday (computed), so Monday 2027-02-01 (DERIVED, not stated by the IRS).
- **A3 Cards and networks.** Same page: payments made with a credit card or payment card and certain other payments, including third-party network transactions, are reported on Form 1099-K by the payment settlement entity and are not reported on Form 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC.
- **A4 Corporations.** Same page: generally no 1099-NEC for payments to a corporation (including an LLC taxed as a C or S corporation), except attorneys' fees (box 1a) and certain federal-agency vendor payments. Reimbursed employee expenses are not reported on 1099-NEC; a travel reimbursement to a nonemployee who did not account to the payer counts toward the total.
- **A5 Truncation.** Same page: all filers may truncate a recipient's TIN on payee statements; truncation is not allowed on documents filed with the IRS; a payer's TIN may not be truncated on any form. The form's own recipient instructions say it may show only the last four digits. (The owner chose to print full TINs on Copy B; see section 2.)
- **A6 Form.** https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/f1099nec--2026.pdf (identical bytes to https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f1099nec.pdf on 2026-10-15): "Form 1099-NEC (Rev. December 2026)", 6 pages, 537,467 bytes, sha256 `68a8f00078be6dd6a4912cc4209df317b31840d5fe19560222d39df9f2f7ff49`; AcroForm with 140 fields (XFA present); copies A, 1, B, 2 each with its own fields; new boxes 1a (nonemployee compensation), 1b cash tips, 1c TTOC, 1d overtime compensation, then 2 (consumer products), 3 (excess golden parachute), 4 (federal income tax withheld), 5-7 state boxes. Copy A "is provided for informational purposes only"; do not print and file Copy A of this PDF; Copy B and the other copies may be printed to furnish the recipient. With 10 or more information returns in the aggregate the filer may be required to e-file (free IRIS portal https://www.irs.gov/iris). The blank is to be pinned in a SEPARATE manifest `data/forms/info-returns/manifest.json` (decision D10; the TY2025 manifest is schema-locked to 2025). Before first use re-check that no superseding revision exists and that the sha256 is unchanged.
- **A7 E-file.** General Instructions for Certain Information Returns (2025 edition read), https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099gi: filers of 10 or more information returns must e-file. Re-confirm the 2026 edition; penalty amounts are deliberately NOT stored.

### Stripe (https://stripe.com/pricing, checked 2026-10-15; fees change, never hard-code in code)

- **A10** Cards and wallets 2.9% + 30 cents per successful domestic card transaction. ACH Direct Debit 0.8%, $5.00 cap. Stripe Invoicing (not used) lists 0.4% per paid invoice on the Starter plan.
- **A11** https://docs.stripe.com/payments/ach-direct-debit: delayed-notification method, up to 4 business days to learn success or failure, minimum amount 0.50 USD, can be disputed. Maximum amounts: **UNVERIFIED** (the owner checks his Stripe dashboard limits).
- **A12** (VERIFIED 2026-10-23) Webhook signatures: https://docs.stripe.com/events/manage-webhook-endpoints.md and https://docs.stripe.com/events/set-up-events.md: verify with the SDK's `constructEvent()` using the exact raw UTF-8 body, the `Stripe-Signature` header (`t=...,v1=...`) and the endpoint secret; frameworks that parse the body break it. The pinned SDK (`stripe` 23.0.1, read from its npm tarball) defaults the tolerance to 300 seconds (`DEFAULT_TOLERANCE`), and passing `0` SKIPS the timestamp check, so the code passes no tolerance argument. `constructEvent()` reads SNAPSHOT events (full `data.object`); Stripe now recommends thin events for new integrations, which this build does not read (a thin event is stored as "unsupported_format", never paid). Snapshot payloads can be stale, so the Checkout Session is re-fetched before a payment is recorded.
- **A14** (VERIFIED 2026-10-23) SDK and API version: `pnpm view stripe version` = 23.0.1 (published 2026-10-22); its `ApiVersion` is `2026-09-30.endive` (pinned in `lib/invoicing/stripe/client.ts`; a test compares the constant with the installed SDK); stripe 23 needs Node 20 or later.
- **A15** (VERIFIED 2026-10-23) Checkout Session create, https://docs.stripe.com/api/checkout/sessions/create.md: in this API version the create call has `allowed_payment_method_types` (a filter on the methods enabled in the Dashboard) and no static `payment_method_types`, so Cards and US bank account must be ENABLED in the owner's Dashboard (https://docs.stripe.com/payments/ach-direct-debit/accept-a-payment.md?payment-ui=checkout; line items in `usd`). `expires_at` may be 30 minutes to 24 hours after creation (default 24 hours); the build uses 2 hours from the start of a 10-minute idempotency bucket. The session carries `payment_status` (`paid` | `unpaid` | `no_payment_required`), `amount_total`, `currency`, `livemode`, `metadata`, `client_reference_id`, `payment_intent`.
- **A16** (VERIFIED 2026-10-23) Fulfilment, https://docs.stripe.com/checkout/fulfillment.md?ui=full-page: fulfilment may run several times, possibly concurrently, for one session; check `payment_status`; delayed methods (ACH) need `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, and `async_payment_failed` can be used to contact the customer. ACH typically takes 4 business days; microdeposit verification can fail as `async_payment_failed`; test-mode ACH settles instantly. Minimum charge 0.50 USD (https://docs.stripe.com/currencies.md).
- **A17** Owner-stated 2026-10-23 (owner statements, not checked against the Stripe or Vercel dashboards by this build): the restricted key has the permissions this plan lists (Checkout Sessions write, PaymentIntents read, Charges read, Balance transactions read where the Dashboard lists it); the webhook destination uses SNAPSHOT events; payouts go to EK Consulting checking; the Vercel project's Node.js Version is 24.x (meets stripe 23's Node 20+; the owner saw a "settings differ from current Production deployment" banner, so the next deploy picks it up). Still UNVERIFIED: the exact Dashboard permission names; the webhook destination's API version (not stated); whether Cards and US bank account are both enabled as payment methods in the Dashboard (not stated); Stripe's behaviour when an idempotency key is reused with different parameters (the build makes them deterministic); ACH maximum amounts.

## 6. Phase 2 as built: the client's link and the Stripe pay link (2026-10-23)

- Tables (migration `20261023000000_invoicing_stripe`, additive: `BillingAccessToken`, `StripeEvent`; `InvoicingProfile.stripePayEnabled` (default false) and `publicTokenTtlDays` (default 90); four nullable `stripe*` columns on `BillingPayment` with a UNIQUE `stripePaymentIntentId`; no CHECK added; NOT applied until the owner OKs the push). The Phase-2 columns are read only by `lib/invoicing/pay-build.ts`, which answers "not set up" on a missing table or column (P2021 / P2022), so every Phase-1 page keeps working before the migration.
- The client's link `/i/<token>`: a 256-bit token, only its sha256 hash stored; valid until `expiresAt` (7 to 365 days, default 90) unless revoked; every invalid case shows the same neutral sentence. A NEW link is minted for every e-mail send (only when online payments apply), and the owner can create (shown once), copy, replace or revoke links on the invoice page (owner-made links also work while online payments are off: view and PDF only). The page shows the invoice, totals, paid and balance, a PDF download and "Pay online"; never an EIN, internal notes, contacts, GL codes or Stripe ids; bank text only when the invoice ticks it. The owner's authenticated PDF download carries no pay link (the invoice page says so); the e-mailed PDF and the PDF on the client's page do.
- Public surfaces (the sanctioned exceptions): middleware `PUBLIC_PATHS` gains exactly `/i` and `/api/stripe/webhook`; every export of `actions/public-invoice.ts` starts with the token gate; `/i/*` sends `no-referrer` and `no-store`; Sentry scrubs `/i/<token>` and the `Stripe-Signature` header; the install prompt hides on `/i`.
- Pay online: a Checkout Session for the CURRENT balance read on the server (card and US bank account, one USD line "Invoice <number>", our invoice and entity ids in the metadata and as `client_reference_id`, static success / cancel pages that carry no token, an idempotency key per invoice, balance and 10-minute bucket). Offered only when the toggle is on, Stripe is configured, `NEXTAUTH_URL` gives a usable origin (https in live mode), the invoice is issued and at least $0.50 is due. Partial online payments, surcharges, refunds and Stripe Invoicing are not built.
- Webhook `POST /api/stripe/webhook`: raw body and signature first (400 otherwise), livemode must match `STRIPE_MODE`, five event types; a payment is recorded only when the re-fetched session is `paid` (cards on `completed`, ACH on `async_payment_succeeded`), once per PaymentIntent, as an ordinary `BillingPayment` (`source` stripe, `method` card / ach, gross amount, fee from the balance transaction or "unknown"), applied up to the invoice's balance; any rest is a deposit on account with a flag. ACH failures, refunds and disputes only flag; nothing is ever reversed automatically. Unresolvable events are stored as unmatched and shown on the "Online payments" page.
- Payouts: Stripe pays out net of fees, several payments per payout; the owner links one bank deposit to several Stripe payments in one step on the "Online payments" page, only once every fee is known ("Fetch fee" reads it once, never after a deposit link).
- The ACH payment date is the day Stripe reports success (up to about 4 business days after the client's click).
- Test-mode clean-up (task 2.7): a Stripe TEST-mode run uses the one shared production database, so every test payment is recorded as an ordinary `BillingPayment` (source `stripe`) with its applications and shows in A/R, the derived ledger and the cash view; only `StripeEvent.livemode = false` marks it as a test, nothing on the invoice or payment pages does, and the test invoice takes a real invoice number (3100 and up, never reused). After the owner's test run: remove each test payment's applications (the "Remove" button beside each application, `removeApplication`), then void each test payment (`voidPayment`; it is refused while an application is active), void the test invoice, and never link a test payment to a bank deposit or a Stripe payout. A "Test mode" marker on such payments is a recorded follow-up (no migration needed: join through `StripeEvent.paymentId`).
